# SOP-MED: Passenger medical emergencies
Code: SOP-MED
Category: medical_emergency
Source: SkyShield sample SOP (synthetic, for development). Not official DGCA text.

## 1. Scope
Serious illness or injury of a passenger on board that needs medical help, use of the emergency medical kit or defibrillator, or a diversion.

## 2. Response
Cabin crew assess the passenger, ask for a doctor on board and inform the captain, who decides on diversion with ground medical support.

## 3. Immediate measures
- MUST: Inform the captain and request ground medical services to meet the aircraft on arrival.
- MUST: Record all treatment given and medical kit items used in the cabin log.
- MUST: Replenish the emergency medical kit and defibrillator before the next flight.
- SHOULD: Collect the contact details of any doctor who assisted on board.

## 4. Preventive measures
- SHOULD: Run a first aid and defibrillator refresher for the cabin crew involved.
- SHOULD: Review the diversion decision time against company guidance.
