# SOP-CRW: Crew incapacitation and fatigue
Code: SOP-CRW
Category: crew_incapacitation
Source: SkyShield sample SOP (synthetic, for development). Themes follow DGCA NASP 2024-2028 in-flight crew incapacitation focus area. Not official DGCA text.

## 1. Scope
Partial or total incapacitation of a flight crew member in flight, including illness, food poisoning, fainting, and severe fatigue affecting performance.

## 2. Contributing factors
Fatigue from roster patterns, dehydration, undeclared medical conditions, and common meals for both pilots.

## 3. Immediate measures
- MUST: The remaining pilot takes control, declares an emergency and lands at the nearest suitable airport.
- MUST: Arrange a medical examination of the incapacitated crew member and remove them from duty until declared fit.
- MUST: Review the crew member's duty and rest records for the previous 7 days for flight duty time limitation compliance.
- SHOULD: Collect meal details served to the crew for possible food poisoning.

## 4. Preventive measures
- MUST: Pilots must not eat the same meal on a flight.
- SHOULD: Review roster patterns with many early starts against the fatigue risk management system.
- SHOULD: Encourage voluntary fatigue reports without penalty.
