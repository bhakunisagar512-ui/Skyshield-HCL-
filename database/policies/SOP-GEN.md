# SOP-GEN: Occurrence reporting and classification
Code: SOP-GEN
Category: general
Source: SkyShield sample SOP (synthetic, for development). Structure follows ICAO Annex 13/19 and DGCA occurrence-reporting principles. Not official DGCA text.

## 1. Purpose
This procedure defines how any safety occurrence is reported, classified and closed. The aim of reporting is prevention, not blame. Reports made in good faith are protected under the just culture policy.

## 2. Who must report
Flight crew, cabin crew, aircraft maintenance engineers, ground handling staff, ramp supervisors and air traffic liaison staff must report any occurrence that endangered, or could have endangered, an aircraft, its occupants or any other person.

## 3. Severity classification
- Accident: a person is fatally or seriously injured, the aircraft sustains damage or structural failure that adversely affects its strength or performance, or the aircraft is missing.
- Serious incident: an incident with a high probability of an accident, such as a near collision, a rejected take-off at high speed, a runway excursion without major damage, fire or smoke, or crew incapacitation.
- Incident: any other occurrence that affects or could affect safe operation.

## 4. Reporting timelines
- MUST: Notify the Safety Office immediately by phone for any accident or serious incident, and file the written report in SkyShield within 24 hours.
- MUST: File any other incident report within 72 hours of the occurrence.
- MUST: Preserve flight data recorder and cockpit voice recorder data after any accident or serious incident; pull the relevant circuit breakers once the aircraft is safe.
- SHOULD: Attach a factual timeline and the names of witnesses in the description.

## 5. Investigation and closure
- MUST: The Safety Officer reviews every report, confirms the severity and records an analysis before measures are decided.
- MUST: Every measure that is not applied must carry a written reason.
- SHOULD: Share de-identified lessons learned in the monthly safety bulletin.
