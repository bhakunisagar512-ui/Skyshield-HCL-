# SOP-OTH: Other occurrences and unruly passengers
Code: SOP-OTH
Category: other
Source: SkyShield sample SOP (synthetic, for development). Not official DGCA text.

## 1. Scope
Occurrences that do not fit another category, including unruly passenger behaviour, dangerous goods found in baggage, security-related events and cabin safety issues such as a slide deployment.

## 2. Immediate measures
- MUST: For unruly passengers, follow the escalation levels in the cabin crew manual and inform the captain; hand the passenger to security on arrival.
- MUST: For undeclared dangerous goods, isolate the item, inform the captain and report to the dangerous goods focal point.
- MUST: For an inadvertent slide deployment, ground the aircraft until the slide is replaced or the door is placarded as per the MEL.
- SHOULD: Collect written statements from the crew and witnesses.

## 3. Preventive measures
- SHOULD: Review the event in the next cabin crew recurrent training.
- SHOULD: Check whether a similar event happened in the last 12 months.
