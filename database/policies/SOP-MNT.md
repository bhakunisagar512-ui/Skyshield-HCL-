# SOP-MNT: Deficient maintenance
Code: SOP-MNT
Category: deficient_maintenance
Source: SkyShield sample SOP (synthetic, for development). Themes follow DGCA NASP 2024-2028, N-HRC 8 Deficient maintenance. Not official DGCA text.

## 1. Scope
Errors in maintenance tasks, missed inspections, wrong parts fitted, panels or caps left open, incorrect release to service, and repeat defects not rectified.

## 2. Contributing factors
Night shift fatigue, incomplete shift handover, missing or outdated task cards, lack of independent inspection and time pressure before departure.

## 3. Immediate measures
- MUST: Re-inspect the affected system and any work done in the same maintenance package.
- MUST: Quarantine the suspect parts and verify part numbers and certificates.
- MUST: Review the certificate of release to service and the engineer's authorisation for the task.
- SHOULD: Check the tooling used for calibration status.

## 4. Preventive measures
- MUST: Apply a duplicate (independent) inspection for any task that disturbs flight controls or engine controls.
- MUST: Use a written shift handover log for every task not completed in a shift.
- SHOULD: Review engineer duty hours for fatigue against the fatigue management policy.
- SHOULD: Add the error to human factors recurrent training as a case study.
