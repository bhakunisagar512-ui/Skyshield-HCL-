# SOP-REX: Runway excursion prevention and response
Code: SOP-REX
Category: runway_excursion
Source: SkyShield sample SOP (synthetic, for development). Themes follow DGCA NASP 2024-2028, N-HRC 3 Runway excursions. Not official DGCA text.

## 1. Scope
Covers veer-offs and overruns during take-off or landing, and high-energy landings, long flare or late touchdown that came close to an excursion.

## 2. Contributing factors
Unstable approaches, tailwind, wet or contaminated runways, long touchdown, late or incorrect use of reversers and autobrake, and hydroplaning in monsoon conditions.

## 3. Immediate measures
- MUST: Inspect the aircraft for landing gear, tyre and brake damage, and inspect runway edge lights and signage for damage.
- MUST: Preserve flight data and download touchdown point, ground speed and braking data for analysis.
- MUST: Remove the crew from roster pending the safety investigation interview, as per the just culture policy.
- SHOULD: Request runway friction measurement and rubber deposit check from the airport operator.

## 4. Preventive measures
- MUST: Enforce the go-around policy: any approach not stabilised by 1,000 ft above aerodrome level must be discontinued.
- MUST: Calculate landing distance with the actual runway condition report before every landing on a wet or contaminated runway.
- SHOULD: Include crosswind and wet-runway landings with late touchdown in the next recurrent simulator session.
- SHOULD: Review the monsoon operations circular with all crews before June each year.
