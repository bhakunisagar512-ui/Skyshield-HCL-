# SOP-RMP: Ramp safety and ground handling
Code: SOP-RMP
Category: ramp_safety
Source: SkyShield sample SOP (synthetic, for development). Themes follow DGCA NASP 2024-2028, N-HRC 7 Ramp safety. Not official DGCA text.

## 1. Scope
Ground handling damage, ground support equipment contact with aircraft, pushback and towing incidents, jet blast, foreign object debris (FOD) on the apron, fuel spills and injuries to ground staff.

## 2. Contributing factors
Time pressure during turnaround, missing wing walkers, faulty ground equipment, poor apron lighting at night and incomplete FOD walks.

## 3. Immediate measures
- MUST: Carry out a damage inspection of the aircraft by a licensed engineer before the next flight after any ground equipment contact.
- MUST: Take the ground support equipment involved out of service until it is inspected.
- MUST: Contain and clean any fuel spill and inform the airport fire service.
- SHOULD: Take photographs of the scene and the equipment positions.

## 4. Preventive measures
- MUST: Use wing walkers and a headset operator for every pushback and towing.
- MUST: Complete a FOD walk on the stand before aircraft arrival.
- SHOULD: Keep ground equipment outside the equipment restraint area until the aircraft has stopped and the anti-collision light is off.
- SHOULD: Run a ramp safety refresher session for the handling team involved.
