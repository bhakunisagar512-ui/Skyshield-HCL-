# SOP-WX: Weather related operations
Code: SOP-WX
Category: weather
Source: SkyShield sample SOP (synthetic, for development). Not official DGCA text.

## 1. Scope
Windshear, microburst, low visibility and fog operations, heavy rain, hail, lightning strike and diversions or go-arounds due to weather below minima.

## 2. Contributing factors
North India winter fog, monsoon thunderstorms, outdated weather information and pressure to land.

## 3. Immediate measures
- MUST: Carry out a lightning strike or hail inspection as per the maintenance manual if the aircraft was struck or flew through hail.
- MUST: Confirm the crew followed the windshear escape manoeuvre on any windshear warning.
- MUST: Verify that the crew held valid low visibility (CAT II/III) qualification if operating in fog below CAT I minima.
- SHOULD: Obtain the METAR and SIGMET reports for the time of the event.

## 4. Preventive measures
- MUST: Do not commence an approach when the reported visibility is below the crew and aircraft minima.
- SHOULD: Plan extra fuel for holding and diversion during the fog season (December to February).
- SHOULD: Share windshear reports from crews with ATC and other operators.
