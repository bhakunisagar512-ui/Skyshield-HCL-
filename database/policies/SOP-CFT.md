# SOP-CFT: Controlled flight into terrain prevention
Code: SOP-CFT
Category: cfit
Source: SkyShield sample SOP (synthetic, for development). Themes follow DGCA NASP 2024-2028, N-HRC 2 CFIT. Not official DGCA text.

## 1. Scope
Any EGPWS or TAWS warning (terrain, pull up, too low gear, sink rate), descent below minimum safe altitude, or approach below the published glide path, especially in hilly terrain such as the north-east, Himalayan and Western Ghats regions.

## 2. Contributing factors
Non-precision approaches, wrong altimeter setting, loss of situational awareness, night or low visibility, and incorrect navigation database.

## 3. Immediate measures
- MUST: Download EGPWS data and flight data for the event.
- MUST: Confirm the crew executed the terrain escape manoeuvre on any hard warning.
- MUST: Verify the EGPWS terrain database and navigation database are current on the aircraft.
- SHOULD: Check the approach chart and minimum sector altitude used by the crew.

## 4. Preventive measures
- MUST: Respond to every hard EGPWS warning with immediate escape manoeuvre; do not try to diagnose first.
- MUST: Fly a stabilised continuous descent final approach on non-precision approaches.
- SHOULD: Include terrain-escape manoeuvre practice in the next simulator session.
- SHOULD: Brief terrain and minimum safe altitudes during the approach briefing at hilly airports.
