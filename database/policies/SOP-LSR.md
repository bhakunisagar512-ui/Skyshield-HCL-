# SOP-LSR: Laser interference
Code: SOP-LSR
Category: laser_interference
Source: SkyShield sample SOP (synthetic, for development). Themes follow DGCA NASP 2024-2028 laser interference focus area. Not official DGCA text.

## 1. Scope
Any laser light illuminating the cockpit during any phase of flight, most often during approach and landing at night.

## 2. Hazard
Laser light can cause glare, flash blindness and after-images at a critical phase of flight. Exposure can cause eye injury.

## 3. Immediate measures
- MUST: Report the laser attack to ATC immediately with the location, direction and colour of the beam.
- MUST: Arrange an eye examination by an aviation medical examiner for any crew member exposed before their next duty.
- MUST: Inform the airport operator and local police so the source can be traced.
- SHOULD: Record the approximate position of the source relative to the runway.

## 4. Preventive measures
- MUST: The pilot not exposed should take or keep control; avoid looking at the beam.
- SHOULD: Turn up cockpit lighting to reduce the effect of glare.
- SHOULD: Track repeat locations and share them with the airport and police for awareness drives.
