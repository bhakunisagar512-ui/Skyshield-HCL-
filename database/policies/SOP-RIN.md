# SOP-RIN: Runway incursion prevention
Code: SOP-RIN
Category: runway_incursion
Source: SkyShield sample SOP (synthetic, for development). Themes follow DGCA NASP 2024-2028, N-HRC 6 Runway incursion. Not official DGCA text.

## 1. Scope
Any incorrect presence of an aircraft, vehicle or person on the protected area of a runway, including crossing a lit stop bar, entering the runway without clearance, or a vehicle on the runway strip without clearance.

## 2. Contributing factors
Readback errors, similar call signs, complex taxiway layouts, hotspots, construction work, low visibility and crew distraction during taxi.

## 3. Immediate measures
- MUST: Obtain the ATC recording and transcript of the clearances and readbacks.
- MUST: Interview the flight crew or vehicle driver involved within 48 hours.
- MUST: Suspend the airside driving permit of the vehicle driver until refresher training is complete, where a vehicle was involved.
- SHOULD: Identify whether the location is a published hotspot.

## 4. Preventive measures
- MUST: Never cross a lit red stop bar, even with ATC clearance; query ATC instead.
- MUST: Use the full call sign and read back all runway hold-short and crossing instructions.
- SHOULD: Brief the expected taxi route and hotspots before starting taxi.
- SHOULD: Turn on strobe lights when crossing or entering any runway.
