# SOP-MAC: Airprox and mid-air collision risk
Code: SOP-MAC
Category: mid_air_collision
Source: SkyShield sample SOP (synthetic, for development). Themes follow DGCA NASP 2024-2028, N-HRC 1 Mid-air collision. Not official DGCA text.

## 1. Scope
Airprox events, loss of separation, TCAS resolution advisories (RA), level busts and deviations from cleared altitude.

## 2. Contributing factors
Level busts during climb or descent, incorrect altimeter setting, readback errors, high workload, and late or incorrect response to TCAS advisories.

## 3. Immediate measures
- MUST: File an airprox report and notify ATC on the same day.
- MUST: Download TCAS and flight data for the event window.
- MUST: Verify that the crew followed the TCAS RA immediately and reported it to ATC.
- SHOULD: Obtain radar replay from the air navigation service provider.

## 4. Preventive measures
- MUST: Always follow a TCAS RA, even if it conflicts with an ATC instruction.
- MUST: Cross-check the cleared altitude set in the altitude selector by both pilots.
- SHOULD: Reduce vertical speed to below 1,500 ft per minute in the last 1,000 ft before the cleared level.
- SHOULD: Add level-bust and TCAS RA scenarios to recurrent training.
