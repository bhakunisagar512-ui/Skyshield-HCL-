# SOP-TEC: Technical defects and system malfunctions
Code: SOP-TEC
Category: technical_defect
Source: SkyShield sample SOP (synthetic, for development). Themes follow DGCA defect-reporting principles. Not official DGCA text.

## 1. Scope
Significant system defects in flight or on ground: hydraulic, electrical, pressurisation, flight control, landing gear, fuel and avionics malfunctions, including smoke or fumes in the cabin or cockpit.

## 2. Contributing factors
Repeat defects, deferred items under the minimum equipment list (MEL), component wear and incomplete troubleshooting.

## 3. Immediate measures
- MUST: Record the defect in the technical log and rectify it before the next flight or defer it strictly as per the MEL.
- MUST: Report any major defect, smoke, fumes or fire to the Safety Office the same day.
- MUST: Remove and send the failed component for shop investigation and keep it traceable.
- SHOULD: Download fault messages from the central maintenance computer.

## 4. Preventive measures
- MUST: Trigger a reliability review when the same defect repeats three times on the same aircraft within 30 days.
- SHOULD: Check other aircraft of the fleet for the same defect or part batch.
- SHOULD: Share the defect trend with the manufacturer's field service representative.
