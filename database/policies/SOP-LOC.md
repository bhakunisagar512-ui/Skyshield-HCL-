# SOP-LOC: Loss of control in flight
Code: SOP-LOC
Category: loss_of_control
Source: SkyShield sample SOP (synthetic, for development). Themes follow DGCA NASP 2024-2028, N-HRC 5 Loss of control in flight. Not official DGCA text.

## 1. Scope
Aircraft upset, stall warning, stick shaker, bank angle exceedance, speed excursions, and unintended attitude or flight path deviation.

## 2. Contributing factors
Automation confusion, incorrect autopilot mode, unreliable airspeed, icing, wake turbulence, and manual handling skills.

## 3. Immediate measures
- MUST: Download flight data and check for stall warning, stick shaker and bank angle exceedance.
- MUST: Inspect pitot-static probes and angle of attack sensors if unreliable airspeed was involved.
- MUST: Ground the aircraft for a structural check if the load factor limits may have been exceeded.
- SHOULD: Interview the crew about autopilot and autothrust modes at the time.

## 4. Preventive measures
- MUST: Complete upset prevention and recovery training (UPRT) for the crew involved before return to line flying.
- SHOULD: Announce and cross-check every flight mode annunciator change.
- SHOULD: Practise manual flying in suitable conditions to maintain handling skills.
