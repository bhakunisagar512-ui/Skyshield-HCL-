# SOP-TRB: Turbulence encounters
Code: SOP-TRB
Category: turbulence
Source: SkyShield sample SOP (synthetic, for development). Themes follow DGCA NASP 2024-2028 inflight turbulence focus area. Not official DGCA text.

## 1. Scope
Moderate or severe turbulence, clear air turbulence, convective turbulence near cumulonimbus clouds, and any turbulence that injured passengers or crew.

## 2. Contributing factors
Monsoon convective weather, flying close to thunderstorms, late weather avoidance decisions, and seat belt sign not on in time.

## 3. Immediate measures
- MUST: Arrange medical assistance on arrival for injured passengers or crew and record the injuries.
- MUST: Carry out a severe turbulence structural inspection if severe turbulence or a load factor exceedance was recorded.
- MUST: Inspect the cabin for loose items, damaged overhead bins and galley equipment.
- SHOULD: Send a pilot report (PIREP) about the turbulence to ATC.

## 4. Preventive measures
- MUST: Keep at least 20 nautical miles lateral separation from cumulonimbus cells where possible.
- MUST: Switch the seat belt sign on and secure cabin crew before entering forecast or reported turbulence.
- SHOULD: Advise passengers to keep seat belts fastened whenever seated.
- SHOULD: Use the weather radar tilt and gain correctly during monsoon season.
