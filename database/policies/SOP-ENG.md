# SOP-ENG: In-flight engine shutdown
Code: SOP-ENG
Category: engine_shutdown
Source: SkyShield sample SOP (synthetic, for development). Not official DGCA text.

## 1. Scope
Any in-flight shutdown of an engine, whether commanded by the crew or uncommanded, and any engine flameout, surge or stall.

## 2. Contributing factors
Fuel contamination, component failure, oil system problems, bird ingestion, and incorrect crew identification of the affected engine.

## 3. Immediate measures
- MUST: Ground the aircraft and carry out a borescope and engine inspection as per the engine manual before further flight.
- MUST: Take fuel samples from the aircraft and the refuelling source for contamination testing.
- MUST: Send oil samples and chip detectors for spectrometric analysis.
- MUST: Notify the engine manufacturer and record the event in the engine history.
- SHOULD: Download engine health monitoring data for the previous ten flights.

## 4. Preventive measures
- MUST: Review the engine trend monitoring for the rest of the fleet for similar parameter shifts.
- SHOULD: Practise engine failure identification and single-engine landing in the next simulator session.
